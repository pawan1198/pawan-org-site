* About Me

I am an independent economic researcher and data analyst based in *New Delhi, India*,
with over *7 years of experience* in quantitative social science research.

My work focuses on Indian macroeconomics, agricultural markets, labour statistics,
trade data, and energy economics — using reproducible, code-first workflows.

** Background

- *Research Consultant* — Independent / Freelance
- *OP Jindal Global University* — Research roles
- *IDEAs Advanced Certificate Programme* — Research training
- *Society for Social and Economic Research (SSER)* — Research coordination

** Education & Training

Advanced training in survey sampling methodology, econometrics, and statistical
computing. Completed IGNOU MST-013 coursework in survey sampling theory.

** Domain Expertise

I work with data from:

- Indian Government Surveys: NSS, PLFS (large-scale household surveys)
- Trade Data: DGCI&S, UN Comtrade (HS-code level import/export)
- Agricultural Data: FAOSTAT, Ministry of Agriculture price series
- Energy Data: NITI Aayog ICED, PPAC petroleum statistics
- Macroeconomic Data: IMF WEO, World Bank WDI, RBI, OECD

** What Makes My Work Different

I combine rigorous *survey methodology* with modern *data engineering* — building
pipelines that are fully reproducible, documented in Quarto, and ready for
peer review or client delivery.

Every analysis I deliver includes clean code, structured outputs (RDS, CSV,
publication-quality PDFs), and plain-language interpretation.
